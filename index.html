❓ ML6CMG Chord Finder Help

🎵 Chord Finder

Select a key from the Circle of Fifths, then choose the chord type you want. The app shows the chord notes, treble-clef notation and instrument diagrams.

⭕ Circle of Fifths

The outer ring contains major keys. The inner ring contains their relative minor keys. Tap a major or minor key to change the selected chord root.

🎸 Instrument diagrams

Use the instrument buttons to show or hide Guitar, Ukulele, Mandolin and Piano. Stringed-instrument diagrams show the actual strings, tuning, open/muted strings and finger numbers.

Finger numbers: 1 Index • 2 Middle • 3 Ring • 4 Little.

🎼 Treble clef

Every note belonging to the selected chord is displayed on the staff and the note names are shown underneath.

🎵 Altered guitar tunings

The guitar can be viewed in Standard, Drop D, DADGAD and DADF#AD. The tuning changes the guitar diagram without changing the underlying chord formulas.

📄 Create Chord Sheet

Add multiple chords to a separate chord-sheet screen. You can use common chords, expand the larger chord library, or type a chord name such as C, Am, F#m7 or Bbmaj7. Choose which instruments appear on the sheet.

📋 Copy / 📤 Share / 🖨️ Print

The chord sheet can be copied, shared through your device's sharing options where supported, or printed/saved as a PDF.

⚠️ Report a possible wrong diagram

If you spot a chord fingering that looks wrong, press and hold the diagram itself for 4 seconds. A report confirmation will appear. The report is prepared for ml6musicgroup@yahoo.com and includes the chord, instrument and tuning so it can be checked and corrected.

Please only report diagrams you genuinely believe contain an error.

ML6 Community Music Group • ML6CMG Chord Finder

🎵 ML6CMG Chord Finder

The ML6CMG Chord Finder is designed to help musicians find chords, understand the notes within them and see practical fingerings for different instruments.

⭕ Circle of Fifths

Select a major or relative minor key directly from the Circle of Fifths.

🎼 Chord Library

Choose from common chords, suspended chords, sevenths, ninths, elevenths, thirteenths and altered chords.

🎸 Instrument Diagrams

The finder can display diagrams for:

  • Guitar
  • Ukulele
  • Mandolin
  • Piano

Stringed-instrument diagrams show the correct strings, frets and finger numbers.

🎼 Music Staff

The treble-clef display shows the notes contained within the selected chord.

🎵 Chord Sheet Creator

You can build a chord sheet by selecting multiple chords and instruments. The finished sheet can contain the chord name, music notation and instrument diagrams.

🔧 Altered Guitar Tunings

Alternative guitar tunings include:

  • DADGAD
  • DADF#AD
  • Drop D

⚠️ Found a Wrong Chord?

If you believe a diagram is incorrect, press and hold the diagram for approximately four seconds. A feedback message will appear allowing you to report the chord for review.

Feedback: ml6musicgroup@yahoo.com

Please include the instrument, chord and tuning when reporting an issue if possible.
